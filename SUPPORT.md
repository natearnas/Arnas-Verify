# Support

Arnas Verify is provided **as-is**, with **no support obligation, no
warranty, and no service-level commitment** of any kind. See the
[LICENSE](LICENSE) "No Liability" clause.

- **Bug reports and questions:** open a
  [GitHub issue](https://github.com/natearnas/Arnas-Verify/issues). Issues
  are answered on a best-effort basis, as time permits.
- **Do not post real license documents, production public keys, or any
  private keys in issues.** Reproduce problems with the demo material in
  `demo_keys/` and `examples/` instead.
- **Commercial support, integration help, and licensing:** Arnas
  Technologies, LLC via <https://arnastech.com>.
